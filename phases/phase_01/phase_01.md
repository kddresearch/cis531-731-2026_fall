## 📖 Phase Overview & Objectives

* **Core Pedagogical Theme:** Establishing rigorous foundations in reproducible data science, distributed computing via Apache Spark, software engineering hygiene, and containerized tooling environments (Phase 1 of the OSEMN framework).
* **Included Modules:** 
  * [`Module 00`](../../modules/module_00/): Welcome & Course Overview (Lecture 00)
  * [`Module 01`](../../modules/module_01/): PySpark Intro: RDDs & DataFrames; Projects, Experiment Design (Lectures 01–04)
  * [`Module 02`](../../modules/module_02/): Cloud Tools, DevContainers & AI/LLM Tooling (Lectures 05–07)
* **Phase Deliverables / Milestones:** Track Selection Briefing, Homework 1 (`HW 1` released & due), and Machine Problem 2 (`MP 2` released).
