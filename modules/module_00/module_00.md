## [Lecture 00: Welcome, Track Selection Briefing & Course Overview](../../lectures/lecture_00.md) (Mon 24 Aug 2026)

1. **Course Orientation & Syllabus Walkthrough:** Comprehensive review of course logistics, Canvas LMS navigation, instructor office hours, and grading distribution across a 1000-point scale.
2. **Track Selection & Responsive Analytics Mandate:** Detailed briefing on the Fall 2026 architectural mandate prohibiting legacy sentiment datasets (Yelp/SEC) and establishing the two immutable tracks: ASR Stream Ingestion (Track 1) and Vector Database & Trope Ontologies (Track 2).
3. **Undergraduate vs. Graduate Differentiation:** Clarification of section-specific technical expectations distinguishing CIS 531 (Undergraduate / Tier 1 Golden Path / Unmodified Schema) from CIS 731 (Graduate / Tier 2 Real-World API/Benchmark / Custom Ontology & Schema Derivation).
4. **FAIR Guiding Principles:** Introduction to Wilkinson et al.'s framework for Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable data management in scalable data science pipelines.
5. **Reproducible Data Science Workflow:** Overview of workflow best practices, environment consistency, and project structure as outlined by Danchev and supporting Jupyter notebook documentation.
* **Assigned Reading:** [Wilkinson et al., *The FAIR Guiding Principles*](https://www.nature.com/articles/sdata201618); [Danchev, *Reproducible Data Science with Python* (Intro & Workflow)](https://valdanchev.github.io/reproducible-data-science-python/intro.html); [Jupyter Notebook Documentation](https://jupyter-notebook.readthedocs.io/)
