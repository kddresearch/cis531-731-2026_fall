2. Track Differentiation: CIS 531 vs. CIS 731 Requirements

CIS 531 (Undergraduate Track)

  • Data Source Tier (Q3): Must use a verified 🟢 Tier 1 (Golden Path) source (Databricks samples.wanderbricks / samples.tpcds_sf1, HF wikimedia/wikipedia / librispeech_asr, Open-Meteo/NOAA, USGS GeoJSON, or whitelisted ASR stream).
  • Schema Authority (Q1 & Q4): Adopt the instructor-provided ontology and chunk schema unmodified, propose a suitable validated third-party schema (subject to instructor approval), or infer a schema that the instructor approves.
  • Container & Kernel Baseline (Q4 & Q5): Standard CPU DevContainer, in-memory FAISS IndexFlatL2 / IndexFlatIP, and fixed cohort kernels in MP 9.

CIS 731 (Graduate & Mixed 531/731 Track)

  • Data Source Tier (Q3): Expected to use a 🟡 Tier 2 (Real-World API / Benchmark) source with raw Bronze payload retention and documented licensing.
  • Schema Authority (Q1 & Q4): Must report a custom ontology and JSON Schema (Draft 2020-12) (class hierarchy, relations, cardinalities, version change log since PS 6, and competency questions). Reusing the unmodified instructor schema forfeits schema credit.
  • Container & Kernel Extensions (Q4 & Q5): Conditional GPU/CPU pull, faiss.write_index / read_index disk persistence, and learned 1-D PyTorch conv kernel plan for MP 9. (In Mixed teams, CIS 731 members must claim these tasks).

3. Detailed Question-by-Question Grading Rubric (30 Points)

Question 1 (Section 1A): Team, 3x5x5 Taxonomy & Track Metadata

2.0 Points

Criteria Audited: (1) Full team roster & section composition (0.5 pts); (2) Complete 3x5x5 coordinate (Analytical Category, Media/Topic Row, and 1–2 claimed OSEMN stages per member + automation plan for unclaimed stages) (0.5 pts); (3) Confirmation of fixed ASR or VDB track from PS 4 (0.5 pts); (4) Schema authority confirmation (CIS 731 custom ontology version & change log; CIS 531 unmodified instructor schema or instructor-approved schema for greenlit proposed project) (0.5 pts).

Tier Points Grading Anchor
Exemplary 2.0 All 4 elements are explicitly stated; per-member OSEMN stage ownership and CIS 731 custom schema version/change log (or CIS 531 unmodified/greenlit confirmation) are unambiguous.
Proficient 1.5 Roster, fixed track, and 3x5x5 coordinates are present, but OSEMN delegation across teammates or the schema version/sourcing note is slightly incomplete.
Developing 1.0 Omits one 3x5x5 dimension, fails to assign individual OSEMN stages, or CIS 731 team omits custom ontology version/change log.
Unacceptable 0.0–0.5 Missing metadata, unauthorized track switch from PS 4 (fixed primary category / topic violation), or CIS 731 team uses the unmodified instructor schema or raw third-party schema without customization.

Question 2 (Section 2): Scope Delta & Proposal Responses

4.0 Points

Criteria Audited: (1) Updated, technically precise problem statement & analytical goal (1.5 pts); (2) Itemized Scope Delta table/list diffing PS 4 vs. PS 8 with technical/feasibility justification for every change or narrowing (1.5 pts); (3) Direct point-by-point responses to instructor PS 4 comments, or proactive self-audit against the Data Sourcing Guide checklist if PS 4 feedback was pending (1.0 pt).

Tier Points Grading Anchor
Exemplary 4.0 Crisp, falsifiable problem statement; itemized before/after Scope Delta with concrete engineering justifications; complete point-by-point resolution of all PS 4 feedback (or thorough self-audit).
Proficient 3.0–3.5 Problem statement and scope changes are clear, but justifications are mostly narrative rather than tied to concrete data/schema metrics, or one minor feedback item is only partially addressed.
Developing 2.0–2.5 Generic assertion that "nothing changed" despite visible shifts in data/methods, or superficial responses to proposal feedback without concrete pipeline adjustments.
Unacceptable 0.0–1.5 Missing problem statement, unacknowledged abandonment of PS 4 scope, or zero engagement with PS 4 feedback / sourcing checklist.

Question 3 (Section 3): Data Provenance, Exploratory Figures & Preliminary Baselines

8.0 Points (Survey) / 10.0 Points (PDF w/ Q6)

Criteria Audited:
• 3A. Data Provenance & Licensing Table (2.5 pts): Exact source ID, whitelist_id / license_class (public_domain or instructor_provided; zero Yelp/SEC data), dataset size, Source Tier (Tier 1/2/3), and repeatable Obtain → Bronze → Silver ingest method.
• 3B. Exploratory Figures (≥3 Wilke-Compliant Figures) (3.5 pts): (i) UMAP sensitivity sweep over n_neighbors and min_dist with fixed random_state and topological interpretation (1.5 pts); (ii) Cohort/drift plot with explicit uncertainty bands (1.0 pt); (iii) Third domain/track diagnostic plot (0.5 pts); (iv) Compliance with Wilke Parts I–II (colorblind-safe palettes, redundant coding, legible labels) (0.5 pts).
• 3C. Preliminary/Naive Baseline & Advisory Metric (2.0 pts): Executed naive/heuristic or MP 7 CPU fixed-demo baseline reporting the track-appropriate metric (Macro-F1, P@k/Recall@k/MRR/nDCG@k, or NIST WER) explicitly marked advisory (grading_eligible=False).

Tier Points Grading Anchor
Exemplary 7.5–8.0 Complete provenance table with verified license_class/whitelist IDs and Bronze-to-Silver handoffs; ≥3 Wilke-compliant figures including a multi-parameter UMAP sweep (n_neighbors × min_dist, fixed seed) and a cohort drift plot with uncertainty bands; working baseline metric reported and marked advisory).
Proficient 6.0–7.0 Provenance table and baseline metric are solid; includes 3 figures, but UMAP sweep varies only one parameter, uncertainty intervals on the drift plot are missing, or minor Wilke design issues occur.
Developing 4.0–5.5 Provenance table lacks exact identifiers/scale or Bronze storage paths; fewer than 3 figures or single default UMAP plot without parameter sweep; baseline is described verbally without an executed metric.
Unacceptable 0.0–3.5 Uses prohibited Yelp/SEC data or unarchived live scraping; no UMAP or exploratory figures generated; no working baseline code or metric.

Question 4 (Section 4): Architecture, Invariants & Pillar Alignment

5.0 Points

Criteria Audited: (1) End-to-end pipeline architecture (Ingest → Clean/Chunk → Embed/Transcribe & Index → Retrieve/Analyze → Evaluate) justified against claimed OSEMN stages with versioned Bronze → Silver → Gold Delta/Parquet/FAISS handoffs (2.0 pts); (2) Fixed component-version manifest table keyed to the course DevContainer or a pinned Devcontainer (1.5 pts); (3) Concrete contract requirements / invariant properties enforcing schema, duration, license, probability, and split bounds (1.5 pts).

Tier Points Grading Anchor
Exemplary 5.0 Clear end-to-end architecture diagram/spec with explicit Bronze/Silver/Gold artifact names; complete component-version table pinned to the course manifest or the one committed by the team; concrete deal contract excerpts tied to track invariants.
Proficient 3.75–4.5 Architecture and version table are complete, but inter-stage table names are generic or deal invariants are stated only informally without concrete pre/post-condition rules.
Developing 2.5–3.5 Omits component-version manifest table, pipeline diagram skips critical stages, or invariants are unaddressed.
Unacceptable 0.0–2.0 Relies on unversioned local CSVs/manual browser downloads with no coherent pipeline architecture or manifest.

Question 5 (Section 5): Risk Register, Annotation Plan & Validation Strategy

5.0 Points

Criteria Audited:
• 5A. Quantified Risk Register (≥5 Risks + CPU Fallback) (2.0 pts): At least 5 track-specific risks with Likelihood, Impact, Trigger, and Mitigation, explicitly including API/GPU quota exhaustion with automatic fallback to the pinned CPU DevContainer baseline.
• 5B. Work Breakdown & MP 9 / HW 12 Gold/Silver Annotation Plan (2.0 pts): Schedule aligned to MP 9, MP 10, PS 11, and HW 12; specifies the 50–200 labeled gold-example target (respecting MP 9's ≤50-row / ≤2-hour per-student cap), weak-supervision silver scaling (source=silver, ≥1,000 chunks), and IAA protocol (20-object overlap, 2-way/3-way Cohen's κ with 95% CIs, adjudication rules; Jurafsky & Martin SLP3).
• 5C. Validation Fractional Cap (≤1%) & Leakage Prevention (1.0 pt): Explains why capping validation splits (e.g., ≤1% on scaled/solo datasets) and isolating human gold data prevents data leakage, overfitting, and evaluation noise (ISLP Ch. 5).

Tier Points Grading Anchor
Exemplary 5.0 ≥5 quantified risks (Likelihood, Impact, Trigger, Mitigation) including mandatory CPU-baseline fallback; complete 50–200 gold + ≥1,000 silver plan with Cohen's κ IAA and adjudication rules; rigorous statistical justification of the ≤1% validation cap.
Proficient 3.75–4.5 Includes 5 risks, CPU fallback, and 50–200 target, but risk triggers are slightly generic or the ≤1% validation cap / silver-vs.-gold separation explanation is brief.
Developing 2.5–3.5 Fewer than 5 risks, omits CPU-baseline fallback, omits IAA overlap/adjudication protocol, or fails to address the validation fractional cap.
Unacceptable 0.0–2.0 Unquantified stub risk list; no viable plan for gold annotation, IAA, or validation split leakage control.

Question 6 (Section 1B): Honor Code & GenAI Role Attestation

2.0 Points

Criteria Audited: Affirms (True in Survey Q6 and verbatim statement in report) that all responses originate from the team's own thoughts, code, and bullet points, and that any Generative AI use was restricted to an editor/synthesizer role in compliance with the GenAI Kit (v1–3.3).

Pass (2.0 pts) Attestation is affirmed (True) and consistent with the uploaded GenAI.pdf audit log.
Fail (0.0 pts) Left blank, answered False, or contradicted by unacknowledged zero-shot AI fabrication of core metrics/artifacts.

Question 7 (Section 7): GenAI Audit Log & Certificate (GenAI.pdf)

2.0 Points

Criteria Audited: Uploads GenAI.pdf in Survey Q7 (and commits to repo) containing: (1) exact prompts used, (2) original pre-AI human draft text/bullet points/schemas ("The Toll"), and (3) raw AI output demonstrating compliance with the Anti-Spooning Stick and Systems Thinking Carrot. (If no AI was used, uploads a PDF explicitly stating "No Generative AI was used for PS 8").

Exemplary (2.0 pts) Complete GenAI.pdf containing exact prompts, pre-AI human bullet points/inputs ("Toll"), and raw AI outputs (or signed zero-AI statement).
Developing (1.0 pt) GenAI.pdf uploaded but omits the student's pre-AI raw bullet points/inputs or truncates prompts.
Missing (0.0 pts) No GenAI.pdf uploaded, or log shows AI hallucinating fake baseline metrics without human data inputs.

Question 8 (Section 6): GitHub Repository Link & ps8-submission Tag

2.0 Points

Criteria Audited: Valid, accessible GitHub repository URL showing: (1) multi-commit history of working code skeleton and baseline implementation, (2) Git tag ps8-submission, and (3) figure generation scripts/sources in interim/.

Exemplary (2.0 pts) Accessible repo URL, active commit history, verified ps8-submission tag, and reproducible figure sources in interim/.
Developing (1.0 pt) Repo URL is accessible with code/figures, but ps8-submission tag is missing or figure sources are outside interim/.
Missing (0.0 pts) Broken/inaccessible URL, empty repository, or zero baseline code/figure commits.
