# PS 4: Term Project Proposal — Grading Rubric (30 Points Total)
**Course:** CIS 531/731 (Fall 2026) | **Instructor:** William H. Hsu

## Scoring Breakdown (30-Point Normalized Scale)

### Part 1: Scope, Taxonomy Alignment & Problem Definition (10 Points / 33.3%)
* **Taxonomy & Problem Statement (10 pts):**
  * **[10 pts]:** Proposal clearly defines an individual submission with team number and member details, explicitly selects one analytical category (Prescriptive, Descriptive, or Predictive) and one media topic (Time-Series, Spatial, Text, Multimedia, or Structured) from the Term Project Taxonomy, and states a concise, well-defined problem statement adhering to the "One Paragraph" team-sharing rule.
  * **[5-9 pts]:** Minor omissions in taxonomy alignment, or problem statement lacks sufficient specificity or violates sharing guidelines.
  * **[0-4 pts]:** Missing category/topic mapping, incoherent problem statement, or shared methodology sections improperly submitted as joint work.

### Part 2: Feasibility & Data Sourcing Check (10 Points / 33.3%)
* **Data Sourcing, Obtain Stage & Methodological Feasibility (10 pts):**
  * **[10 pts]:** Proposal explicitly identifies a Tier 1 or Tier 2 data source (or provides an approved Tier 3 feasibility justification) as outlined in the Data Sourcing & OSEMN Execution Guide. Details the raw landing format, authentication/API limits, minimal acquisition test results, Bronze storage destination, Silver target schema, concrete Scrub cleaning operations, and a simple fallback ("Method 1") baseline model.
  * **[5-9 pts]:** Sources or pipelines are named but lack acquisition test evidence, concrete Scrub cleaning steps, or fallback baseline details.
  * **[0-4 pts]:** Unverified scraping, missing access/authentication plans, or unfeasible tarpit data sources without justification.

### Part 3: Clarity, Structure & Formatting (5 Points / 16.7%)
* **Document Structure & PDF Delivery (5 pts):**
  * **[5 pts]:** Submitted as a clean, 1-to-2 page individual PDF following the exact required section structure (Title & Team, Taxonomy Alignment, Problem Statement, Data Sourcing, Proposed Methodology, and GenAI Audit).
  * **[2-4 pts]:** Minor structural deviations or formatting inconsistencies, but core sections are present.
  * **[0-1 pt]:** Fails page-count limits, missing required named sections, or submitted in an incorrect file format.

### Part 4: Generative AI Verification (5 Points / 16.7%)
* **GenAI Audit & Compliance (5 pts):**
  * **[5 pts]:** Includes the required `GenAI.pdf` interaction log demonstrating compliance with the Generative AI Project Kit and Self-Service Feedback Guide, or provides an explicit, compliant "No GenAI Used" statement.
  * **[2-4 pts]:** Audit log is incomplete or lacks required raw prompt/note disclosures.
  * **[0-1 pt]:** Missing audit log or unverified AI usage without attribution.
