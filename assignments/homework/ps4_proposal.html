PS 4: Term Project Proposal

Project Categories, Topics, and Tasks

The purpose of this assignment is to have you examine project categories (main application use cases), topics (principal data sources and types), and tasks (modeling and analytical inference), select a real-world dataset, and understand the overall goal for your term project. Students are permitted to use their own relevant research problems as the topic of their term projects.

⚠️ CRITICAL: Strict Teaming & Submission Protocol

  • Proposals are INDIVIDUAL: EACH student must upload their proposal as a PDF file with the team number and other team member names (if any) written at the top.
  • The "One Paragraph" Rule: Only one paragraph (the introduction, including the problem statement) should be shared by members of a team. This is to ensure individual accountability for your selected tasks. Shared methodology sections will result in zero points.
  • Future Deliverables: Later, you will finalize your individual commitments and turn in shared interim updates as class participation exercises, along with a shared final presentation and report.

Assignment Workflow & Checklist

If you have not already done so, please read the Term Project Guidelines (especially the list of deliverables and FAQ) and the guide to projects by category, topic, and task.

  • ✅ Step 1: Discussion Draft. Post your draft first as the class participation exercise for Weeks 02-04.
  • ✅ Step 2: Team Assignment. Get your draft approved and respond to feedback in the thread. You should be assigned a team number.
  • ✅ Step 3: GenAI Audit. Consult the course policy on generative AI and other writing/programming tools, and include your disclosure.
  • ✅ Step 4: Final Submission. Upload your final, individualized PDF to this assignment portal by the due date.
