Part 1: Concrete Provenance, Representational-Harm & Pipeline Risk Audit

(15.0 Points Total)

Conduct a rigorous, data-grounded ethics and systems-risk audit of your term project in ethics_memo.md (rendered to ethics_memo.pdf, ≤ 4 pages).

1A. Provenance, Rights & Privacy Audit (6.0 pts)

  • Source Rights & Whitelist Verification: Document the exact license_class (public_domain, instructor_provided, or documented Tier 1/Tier 2 API terms) or whitelist ID for every table/stream in your pipeline (confirming zero Yelp or SEC-filing data, INV-1, and zero reproduction of copyrighted lyrics beyond instructor excerpts).
  • PII Inventory, Redaction & Retention Policy: Enumerate concrete PII or sensitive entity classes present in your raw Bronze payloads (e.g., caller names/locations/phone numbers in call-in speech, user IDs, or precise coordinates), specify your programmatic redaction/hashing method in Scrub, and state your retention/deletion schedule.

1B. Representational Harm, Ecological Fallacy & Cohort Disparity (4.5 pts)

  • Ecological Fallacy & Selection Bias: Analyze the specific ecological-fallacy risk in your project (e.g., why release decade or temporal cohort is not equivalent to audience demographics) alongside survivorship and sampling selection bias in your Bronze/Gold sets.
  • Slice & Modality Error Disparity: Quantify error disparities across cohorts, dialects, or data slices using your MP 10 stratified results (including WER disparity across speaker/acoustic groups for ASR track projects, or Macro-F1/calibration disparity across cohorts for VDB/structured projects).

1C. LLM & Pipeline Security Risks (4.5 pts)

  • Hallucinated Attribution & Uncalibrated Confidence: Analyze how hallucinated trope/label attribution or miscalibrated probabilities could mislead downstream decisions, and specify your concrete mitigation (e.g., constrained verbalizers, source-chunk ID citation checks, temperature scaling, abstention thresholds).
  • Indirect Prompt Injection & Poisoned Inputs: Demonstrate how untrusted transcript segments or retrieved corpus chunks could trigger prompt injection or schema corruption, and specify your implemented sanitization/delimiter defense.
