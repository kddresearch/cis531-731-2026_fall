Part 1: Environment Verification

(30 Points)

You must prove your local Docker DevContainer or Beocat cluster access is correctly configured to run PySpark 4.

Tasks:

  • Initialize a SparkSession using PySpark 4. Define a simple StructType schema containing at least one String and one Integer.
  • Create a dummy DataFrame with 3 rows of data and execute a .show() action.
  • Capture a screenshot of your terminal/Jupyter notebook showing the successful .show() output alongside the Spark Web UI (port 4040) running in your browser.

Part 2: Project Architecture & Matrix Selection

(70 Points)

Objective: Review the "Guide to Projects by Category, Topic, and Task" matrix provided in the course materials to formally establish your team and scope.

  1. Team Roster & Track: List your team members (1-3 students). Declare your immutable data track: Track 1 (ASR Live Stream) or Track 2 (VDB Text Corpora).
  2. Category & Task Allocation: Specify your Category (e.g., Category 2: Descriptive Analytics). For each team member, explicitly claim one Task column (Prediction, Classification, Summarization, Collection Analysis, Retrieval). Note: No team member may own more than one task. If a task is shared, you must state who is building the baseline model vs. the alternative model.
  3. Problem Statement: Write a 3-4 sentence summary of what your pipeline will actually do, combining your Track, Category, and Task allocations.

Deliverable: Combine your Part 1 screenshot and a Markdown (.md) or PDF document containing your Part 2 answers into a single .zip archive named HW1_Firstname_Lastname.zip.
