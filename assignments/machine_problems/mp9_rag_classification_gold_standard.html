Part 1: Gold-Standard Construction, IAA & Weak-Supervision Expansion

(12.0 Points Total)

Construct and verify a held-out gold-standard evaluation set (source="gold", at most 50 objects or 2.0 hours), compute Inter-Annotator Agreement (IAA) on a 20-object partner overlap (overlap_ids.txt), and expand an unlabeled pool of at least 1,000 chunks using weak supervision (source="silver"). Refer to the Term Project Guidelines and Data Sourcing Guide.

1A. Three Valid Pathways for Your ≤ 50-Object Gold Set (source="gold")

Select the pathway that matches your approved project (declare your chosen pathway in attestation.md):

  • Pathway A — Third-Party or Historical Ground-Truth Audit (Default Recommended): Source pre-annotated benchmark labels, historical outcome variables, or reference transcripts in your scripted Obtain stage. Sample ≤ 50 objects (including the 20 shared objects in overlap_ids.txt) and spend your ≤ 2.0-hour human budget auditing and verifying each third-party label/transcript against your codebook rules.
  • Pathway B — Deterministic Join / Rule-Based Alignment Audit (Generally Acceptable): Derive labels by deterministically joining alignable sources via an explicit join key, timestamp window, or verifiable string/regexp matching (generative programming is permitted to write the deterministic join/regexp code). Spend your ≤ 50-object / ≤ 2.0-hour human budget auditing and verifying the alignment on those ≤ 50 objects and adjudicating boundary cases across the 20-object overlap.
  • Pathway C — Authorized Manual Annotation (By Project Approval Only): If explicitly authorized in your project approval to label unannotated raw data (e.g., transcribing ≤ 15-second ASR audio segments or labeling raw text chunks), manually annotate ≤ 50 objects (≤ 2.0 hours) with a 20-object partner overlap.

1B. Codebook, Partner Overlap IAA & Weak-Supervision Pool (≥ 1,000 Chunks)

  • Codebook & Attestation (3.0 pts): Document label definitions, decision rules, boundary cases, and exemplars in codebook.md; log start/stop timestamps, pathway choice, and total human effort (≤ 2.0 hours) in attestation.md.
  • Inter-Annotator Agreement & Adjudication (4.5 pts): Compute Cohen's κ with a 95% bootstrap confidence interval, percent agreement, and the confusion matrix on the 20-object partner overlap (overlap_ids.txt), and document your adjudication rules producing gold_adjudicated.jsonl.
  • Weak-Supervision Expansion (4.5 pts): Apply your MP 7 classifier and at least two simple labeling functions to produce weak-supervision labels (source="silver") on an unlabeled pool of at least 1,000 chunks, aggregate by weighted vote, and evaluate silver-vs-gold agreement and failure modes on your gold objects.

Part 2: Retrieval-Augmented Classification & Cohort Convolution Analysis

(18.0 Points Total: 16.5 pts Technical + 1.5 pts GenAI Verification)

Objective: Evaluate top-k exemplar-conditioned RAG classification against your MP 7 fixed-demonstration baseline and quantify temporal/cohort drift with 95% bootstrap confidence intervals.

  1. Top-k Retrieval-Augmented Classification (7.5 pts): Retrieve top-k exemplars (k ∈ {3, 5, 10}) from your MP 7 FAISS index to condition classification. Report retrieval Precision@k and Recall@k alongside classification Macro-F1 (each with 95% bootstrap CIs on gold_adjudicated.jsonl) and compare against your MP 7 fixed-demonstration baseline. (ASR track additionally reports NIST-normalized WER and Entity Error Rate as implemented in lab08.ipynb.)
  2. Cohort Convolution Analysis (6.0 pts): Bin chunks along your ordered cohort axis (VDB track: release decade / era; ASR track: event-time window index), form per-cohort trope/class frequency vectors, and convolve with a smoothing kernel [0.25, 0.5, 0.25] and a first-difference kernel [-1, 1] to estimate cohort-to-cohort drift with 95% stratified bootstrap CIs. CIS 731 graduate students must additionally fit a learned 1-D convolutional kernel (PyTorch 2.x on CPU) and compare held-out forecast error against the fixed kernels.
  3. Reproducibility, Verification Gate & GenAI Audit (4.5 pts: 3.0 pts Gate + 1.5 pts GenAI): Ensure gold_raw.* has ≤ 50 rows, include .devcontainer/devcontainer.json, verify CPU execution, and include GenAI.pdf.

Graded Metric Separation: Headline evaluation metrics must be computed strictly on your adjudicated gold set (gold_adjudicated.jsonl); metrics on the 1,000-chunk silver pool are advisory only.
