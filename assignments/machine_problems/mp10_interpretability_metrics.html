Part 1: Metrics & Statistical Inference Theory

(9.0 Points Total)

Establish the mathematical and statistical foundations for your evaluation harness before running automated comparisons.

1A. Metric Derivations & Imbalance Counterexample (4.5 pts)

  • Classification & Ranking Derivations: Derive by hand micro-F1, macro-F1, and weighted-F1 from multi-class confusion matrices, alongside retrieval metrics P@k, Recall@k, MRR, and nDCG@k ($k \in \{3, 5, 10\}$). Verify each formula numerically on a worked example from your own confusion matrices.
  • Imbalance Rank-Reversal Counterexample: Construct an explicit numeric counterexample showing two classifiers evaluated on an imbalanced class distribution where raw Accuracy and Macro-F1 rank the two classifiers in opposite orders.
  • ASR Track Requirement: State the formal NIST OpenASR 2020 definition of Word Error Rate ($\text{WER} = \frac{S+D+I}{N_{\text{ref}}}$), specify your text-normalization steps, and prove with a concrete numeric example why $\text{WER}$ can exceed $100\%$.

1B. Inference Design, Assumptions & Pre-Registered BH Family (4.5 pts)

  • Paired Hypothesis Test Selection: State your formal null ($H_0$) and alternative ($H_1$) hypotheses comparing your two best systems (e.g., MP 9 top-k RAG classifier vs. MP 7 fixed-demonstration baseline, or Whisper vs. AssemblyAI on ASR). Justify your choice of paired bootstrap resampling ($B \ge 10,000$), exact McNemar's test (when discordant counts $b+c < 25$), or Wilcoxon signed-rank test, and state the distributional assumptions of each.
  • Pre-Registered Comparison Family ($m \ge 6$): Define your family of at least 6 hypothesis comparisons (e.g., across shot counts, retrieval depths $k \in \{3, 5, 10\}$, feature ablations, or cohort slices) before inspecting test outcomes, and specify the Benjamini–Hochberg (BH) step-up False Discovery Rate control procedure at $\alpha = 0.05$.

Part 2: Scoring Harness, Interpretability, Calibration & Small-n Power Analysis

(21.0 Points Total: 19.5 pts Technical + 1.5 pts GenAI Verification)

Objective: Implement a contract-enforced harness/ package and execute global/local interpretability, probability calibration, BH-corrected paired significance testing, and silver-pool power analysis on the pinned CPU DevContainer.

  1. Scoring Harness & deal Contracts (6.0 pts): Implement evaluate(model, X, y) inside the harness/ package with deal pre-/post-condition contracts verifying that bounded metrics lie in $[0, 1]$, probability rows sum to $1.0$ within $10^{-6}$, confusion-matrix entries sum to $n$, and graded vs. advisory (`grading_eligible=False`) flags are enforced. Include a passing pytest unit-test suite in tests/.
  2. Global & Local Interpretability Analyses (4.5 pts): Run global permutation importance with at least 30 repeats (reporting mean importance drop and 95% CIs) and linear-term contributions where applicable. Generate retrieval-based local explanations (nearest training/exemplar neighbors, similarity scores, and attribution analysis) for at least 10 misclassified examples from your gold set.
  3. Probability Calibration (3.0 pts): Plot reliability diagrams and compute Expected Calibration Error (ECE) under both 10 bins and 15 bins before and after post-hoc calibration (temperature scaling or isotonic regression).
  4. Paired Statistical Comparison & FDR Control (3.0 pts): Execute your paired comparison ($B \ge 10,000$ paired bootstrap or McNemar's test) reporting the point difference $\hat{\Delta}$, 95% CI, raw $p$-value, and Benjamini–Hochberg adjusted $q$-values across your pre-registered family of $\ge 6$ comparisons.
  5. Small-n Power, Stability & Cohort Stratification (3.0 pts): Subsample your MP 9 weak-supervision silver pool (source="silver", grading_eligible=False) across increasing sample sizes $n \in \{25, 50, 100, 250, 500, 1000\}$ to plot 95% CI width vs. $n$. Report cohort-stratified metrics with uncertainty and explicitly state what minimum effect size $\delta$ an evaluation set of $n \le 50$ can and cannot statistically support.
  6. GenAI Verification (1.5 pts): Include your complete GenAI.pdf audit log per GenAI Kit v3.3 (or a 1-page PDF stating "No GenAI Used").
