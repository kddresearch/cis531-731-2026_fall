## Slide 2: The Imperative of Human Ground Truth
### Enforcing Course Invariant INV-17
* **The Epistemic Threat:** Using a large language model (e.g., GPT-5 or Claude) or a massive ASR model (e.g., Whisper Large) to generate your "ground truth" destroys construct validity.
* **Methodological Circularity:** If an AI creates the test, and an AI takes the test, you are measuring *model agreement*, not objective accuracy.
* **INV-17 Mandate:** Every label, transcript, and classification in your evaluation set MUST be generated by a human ear and a human brain.
* **The Audit:** Your GitOps commit logs must reflect human annotation time.
* **The Fiduciary Reality:** In production environments, human validation is required for compliance and legal defensibility.

**Speaker Notes:**
I need to reiterate the absolute most important rule of Phase 5: Course Invariant 17. You cannot use an AI to write your answer key. It is incredibly tempting to take your 50 audio clips, run them through the biggest, slowest Whisper model you can find, and call that the "truth." If you do that, and then evaluate a smaller, faster model against it, you are committing scientific fraud. You are measuring how well the small model mimics the big model's hallucinations. Real science requires an objective, independent baseline. For today's lab, you must put on your headphones, listen to the audio, and type the words yourself.
